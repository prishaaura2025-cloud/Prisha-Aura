About Prisha Aura

Home-based Premium Candle Studio in Pune

Welcome to Prisha Aura, your trusted local destination for premium, 100% handcrafted scented candles and elegant designer candle holders, proudly operating from the heart of Hadapsar and Kalepadal, Pune. We firmly believe that every corner of your home deserves a soothing touch and a beautiful glow.

As a dedicated home studio, we specialize in creating custom-designed candles crafted meticulously by hand. We use high-quality, eco-friendly wax infused with premium therapeutic fragrances that transform your space into a peaceful sanctuary.

Our Specialties & Customizations:

  • Multiple Shapes & Sizes: From elegant jar candles and minimalist pillars to creative aesthetic shapes tailored perfectly to your taste.
  • Exclusive Candle Holders: Beautifully handcrafted holders available in multiple unique designs to elevate your home decor.
  • Occasion-Specific Packs: Specially curated collections perfect for birthdays, intimate parties, corporate gifts and small family celebrations.
  • Festive Collections: Uplit your festive spirit during Diwali, Christmas and other celebrations with our custom decorative candle sets.
